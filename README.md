## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls uses traditional two-dimensional fighting-game movement within 3D anime-inspired stages. Practice walking, crouching, jumping, blocking, backsteps, and character-specific movement in Training Mode. Directional inputs also determine which teammate is called for an assist or selected for switching.

### Tools & Inventory

The game has no conventional inventory, equipment screen, or item-management system. Trainer options apply directly to battle resources such as team health, the Skill Gauge, the Assemble Gauge, assist recovery, and round conditions.

### World & Environment

The game is organized around fights rather than free-roaming exploration. Offline content includes Episode Mode, local Versus, Training Mode, All-Star Arena, Battle Guides, and Character Guides. Episode Mode presents the game's Marvel storyline across three chapters, while battles can include stage transitions caused by Wall Breaks.

### Combat & Weapons

Build a team of four Marvel characters and begin with a lead fighter supported by teammates. Use Light, Medium, and Heavy attacks, Skills, Super Skills, Ultimate Skills, blocking, Assemble Assist attacks, defensive Assemble actions, and character switching. Link Attacks allow accessible combo routes, while advanced routes can combine normal attacks, Skills, Assemble Rush, and follow-up attacks. The trainer can provide unlimited health and resources for practice, but online competitive modes should remain disabled.
